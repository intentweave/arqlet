    Reference and legacy curation

    The view is edited directly: drag a node, a container's header, or a pin; drag a corner to resize; Ctrl or Cmd+Z undoes, with Shift (or Ctrl or Cmd+Y) it redoes. Every change goes through the engine.

    • Available: move (also a selection chosen with Cmd or Ctrl-click), resize, pin, port placement (drag a port along the border), label placement (drag a label), collapse, shape, direction, strategy, routing, undo and redo, reference save and open.
    • Not available in the direct view: moving a route segment, doglegs (Alt-click), route locks (Shift-click), size pins. Opening a reference there uses its baseline view and does not apply these curated parts; the legacy draft below edits and keeps them.
