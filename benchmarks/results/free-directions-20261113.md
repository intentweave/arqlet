Reading:

- **S0, free directions today:** single pass delegates most edges between areas a free direction lays out (org-chart 0 of
  82 built, workflow-dag 0 of 125): a free area decides its orientation only after its children are placed, while the
  border nodes of a child of another axis (ADR 0017) need the parent's axis before. Crossings rise 2–3× against `lr`,
  lines run through nodes (143 on layerweave-code), and layerweave-code takes 53 s instead of 0.5 s.
- **S2 (first version):** the orientations the plain free layout chose by compactness, laid out again fixed. Single pass
  built nearly every edge and crossings fell 40 % against plain `auto` (without layerweave-code 499 / 1504), but stayed
  28 % above `lr`, at twice the time and 56 s on layerweave-code.
- **S2b (this version):** the candidates are every free area left to right and every free area top to bottom, each laid
  out fixed under single pass; the best published key wins (overlaps, lines through nodes, shared lines, crossings, …,
  bends before length). By that key it is never worse than `lr`, which is a candidate. The compact choice of S2 was a
  third candidate and never won here, at the cost of the old free layout, so it is not tried. Time about twice `lr`
  (layerweave-code 1.2 s instead of 0.54 s; plain `auto` 53 s).
- **Not done:** a mixed choice per area under single pass (a greedy flip per area) — none won on these examples, so the
  gain is open; tree, radial and organic areas still keep single pass off for the whole graph (S1).
