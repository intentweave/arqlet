Reading:

- **S0, free directions today:** single pass delegates most edges between areas a free direction lays out (org-chart 0 of
  82 built, workflow-dag 0 of 125): a free area decides its orientation only after its children are placed, while the
  border nodes of a child of another axis (ADR 0017) need the parent's axis before. Crossings rise 2–3× against `lr`,
  lines run through nodes (143 on layerweave-code), and layerweave-code takes 53 s instead of 0.5 s.
- **S2, `directionsFirst`:** a first layout chooses the orientations, a second lays the drawing out with them fixed.
  Single pass then builds nearly every edge (delegated 0 on 11 of 15), lines through nodes vanish except 7 on
  layerweave-code, and crossings fall against plain `auto` on most datasets.
- **Not done:** against fixed `lr` the free directions still cross more on several datasets and are sometimes larger:
  the first layout chooses by compactness (area and length) only, on a drawing single pass did not make. Choosing with
  crossings and with single pass, and a first stage without routing (it costs as much as the old free layout), are the
  next steps. The option stays opt-in.
