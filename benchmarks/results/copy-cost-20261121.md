# Copy cost of the canonical graph, 2026-11-21

Question (ADR 0021, "The shared in-memory graph"): what do the index, the validation, a full copy and a projection of the
canonical graph cost, against reading the graph in place? Tool: `benchmarks/copy-cost` (see its README).

## Machine and build

- Apple M3, 8 logical CPUs, macOS 26.0, rustc 1.99.0 (b940084d7 2026-09-28).
- Profile `release` of the standalone `copy-cost` crate (opt-level 3, no LTO). The engine crates it links are built with
  the same profile.
- Revision 5212261 (`arqlet-model` and `arqlet-view` as of that commit) plus the `benchmarks/copy-cost` files of this
  change, which did not exist at that commit.
- Load average (1 / 5 / 15 min): 3.48 / 7.75 / 8.10 before the run, 3.02 / 6.61 / 7.64 after. The run started once the
  one-minute load fell below 3.5 (waited 204 s); another process had kept the machine busy before. The machine is shared,
  not idle. Two earlier runs at similar load gave the same medians within about 5 %; one run during heavy load (load
  10 to 17) had spreads up to a factor of 4 and was discarded.
- One process, one thread. Each figure: median of 15 runs after one unmeasured warm-up; min and max in the output.

## Results (median, ms)

| Operation | 2k | 20k | 100k | 200k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In place: walk every node and edge by index | 0.01 | 0.06 | 0.35 | 0.80 |
| `GraphIndex::of(&graph)` | 1.47 | 15.03 | 97.65 | 238.32 |
| `arqlet_model::validate(&graph)` | 2.21 | 26.11 | 162.05 | 411.30 |
| `Graph::clone()` (a full copy) | 0.61 | 6.37 | 31.69 | 65.09 |
| `project_indexed`, nothing collapsed (view = whole graph) | 2.79 | 36.18 | 230.50 | 590.54 |
| `project_indexed`, level 1 collapsed | 0.50 | 7.21 | 39.42 | 83.16 |
| `project_indexed`, top level collapsed | 0.25 | 4.34 | 23.85 | 50.38 |
| `project` (validate + index + project), nothing collapsed | 6.03 | 79.26 | 497.13 | 1238.77 |
| `project` (validate + index + project), top level collapsed | 3.37 | 47.51 | 289.96 | 700.82 |

Views: with the top level collapsed the view has 3, 38, 191 and 399 nodes; with level 1 collapsed 34, 349, 1,736 and
3,561 nodes. Spread (max against min) of the figures above: within 10 % for 20k, 100k and 200k, except `GraphIndex::of` at
200k (max 353 ms against a median of 238 ms) and `project` at 200k with the top level collapsed (max 834 ms against 701
ms, min 676 ms). At 2k the values are small and the spread reaches 27 % (`GraphIndex::of`: 1.39 to 1.77 ms).

## Layout time for scale (same machine, same revision)

`target/release/bench --graph FILE --limit 100 --reps N --threads none`, `importMs` (view selection, projection and layout
of the bounded view). `bench` built with `--features benchmark-timings` as `benchmarks/run.sh` does. Load average at the
start of this run: 3.89 / 8.41 / 8.18, at its end 3.90 / 8.33 / 8.15. Spread within 5 %.

| Graph | `importMs` median (samples) | `project` (validate + index + project, top level collapsed) | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| synthetic-2k | 66.68 (5) | 3.37 | about 5 % |
| synthetic-20k | 189.65 (5) | 47.51 | about 25 % |
| synthetic-100k | 777.39 (3) | 289.96 | about 37 % |

The share compares two separate measurements; the import's own collapse set differs from "top level collapsed". It shows
the order of magnitude, not a breakdown of the import.

## What the figures say

- Reading the graph in place costs 0.01 to 0.80 ms. A full copy costs about 60 times that at 2k (0.61 against 0.01 ms) and
  about 80 times at 200k; the index, the validation and a projection of the whole graph cost about 150 to 740 times that.
- `GraphIndex::of` costs 2.4 times a full `Graph::clone()` at 20k nodes, 3.1 times at 100k and 3.7 times at 200k: it builds
  a `BTreeMap<String, usize>` of cloned ids. `validate` costs more still. Both grow faster than the graph (20k to 100k
  nodes is 5.0 times: clone 5.0, index 6.5, validate 6.2; 100k to 200k is 2.0 times: clone 2.1, index 2.4, validate 2.5).
- Projecting a small view is cheap (4 to 50 ms for edges from 61k to 611k, about 70 to 82 ns per source edge); about 90 %
  of the public `project` call with the top level collapsed is validation and indexing, not the projection.
- A projection that keeps the whole graph costs 36 ms at 20k nodes and 591 ms at 200k, six to nine times a plain copy.
- Against the layout time of an import of a bounded view, the validation, index and projection are 5 % at 2k, about 25 % at
  20k and about 37 % at 100k graph nodes.

## What the figures do not say

- Nothing about layout of larger views, routing, memory use, or the share of time on other graphs than this generator's.
- Nothing about an engine that reads the graph through a view trait: the "in place" row is a loop over the existing
  vectors and the index, a lower bound, not a prototype of the query adapter.
- A single machine, a shared one, a single thread; the loads are recorded above.
