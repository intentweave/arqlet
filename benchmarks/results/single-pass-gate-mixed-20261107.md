# Single pass on the mixed datasets against the default gate, 2026-11-07

Revision `b8e9c98` plus the option `orderSeed`, Apple M3, release builds. The 11 datasets with mixed `lr`/`tb` containers, unmodified
(raw task), cut to 150 nodes, as `singlePassAuto` would meet them if its pre-check let them through. Both arms run with `orderSeed`
0–4; every metric is the mean over the five seeds. Default: import and refine (the refined default). Single pass: explicit
`abstractRouting` + `singlePass`. Per view, lexicographic in the order used by the earlier single-pass reports (sibling overlaps,
line–node hits, shared line pairs, crossings, hugging pairs, bends, length). No time is claimed here.

Raw: [single-pass-gate-mixed-20261107.json](single-pass-gate-mixed-20261107.json) (with `perSeed`). Command:
`node benchmarks/eval/single-pass.mjs --corpus --raw --repeat 1 --seeds 5 --arms default,singlePass --no-elk DATASETS…`.

## The seed option

`orderSeed` (provisional, `LayoutOptions`, omitted at 0) seeds the shuffled starts of the hierarchical order, its runs from the
fourth on. Seed 0 is the order used before the option existed: the default is byte-identical on all 31 datasets at threads 1 and
4, and single pass on every uniform view (layerweave-code was regenerated by a playground build in between, 355 → 353 edges in its
view, so it is not comparable). Earlier five-seed means came from an uncommitted copy that read the seed from the environment;
`--seeds N` in `benchmarks/eval/single-pass.mjs` replaces it.

## Reading

Against ADR 0016 the mixed datasets cannot go to single pass in `singlePassAuto` yet: three views are worse than the default
without a documented reason. Two of them are close (uml-class by 0.2 crossings in the mean, deployment-cluster by 5.6 where the
default's own spread is 62); cloud-architecture is worse on every seed. Bends and length remain well above the default's on every
mixed view; they rank below crossings and hugging, so they decide no verdict here.

## Follow-up: ports in the order of their chain's far end, and side lanes by order

Against `fa3a3b3`, same command (five seeds).

**Cause on cloud-architecture.** The sweep's model and the drawing agreed inside the vpc (16 crossings each) but not around it:
the region drew 13 crossings where the model counted 9. The four extra were crossings inside bundles. The edges of one chain
(monitoring ← vpc, queue ← vpc) left their leaf in the order of their ids as text (`e15`, `e21`, `e9`), because a leaf's port took
the border place of the chain's next element, a dummy, which has none. Their border nodes in the vpc lie in another order
(`e21`, `e9`, `e15`), so the edges crossed each other on the chain.

**Changes (single pass only; the default is byte-identical on all 31 datasets at threads 1 and 4):**

1. A leaf's port takes the border place of the container at the far end of its chain, also across dummies.
2. That made the chains of containers with side nodes inconsistent: the leaf's ports followed the side ports, the chain's slots
   still the ids (org-chart 70 → 82). The placement now gets lanes for a child's side edges in the side ports' order (by the
   side node's rank, then the first of a rank farthest along), spread evenly over the child. The exact port places stay out:
   taking them aligned other lanes onto them (rejected above, in the bends report).

## The trade across area borders on cloud-architecture

Against `e12c88f`. Over 20 seeds single pass draws 35–38 crossings on cloud-architecture (default: 31 on every seed), so
the sweep's search is not the limit. With the default's orders of the vpc and the region forced into single pass (a temporary
debug hook, not committed) single pass draws exactly what its model predicts, region 5 and vpc 21, 37 in all, against 36 with
its own orders: the model is right and the sweep's trade is the better one for single pass's structure.

At equal orders the gap to the default comes from one node's edges: the four edges of db-a (e28, e29 in; e30, e31 out) cross
each other 9 times in single pass and 4 times in the default. db-a lies in data-a (lr) in az-a (tb) in the vpc (lr). All four
leave az-a through side nodes in the rank after data-a, at the bottom of az-a, run to its lower right corner and back up through
the vpc to their ends (e28: 14 bends; the default: 2, straight out of the right side). This is the limit of the port model of
ADR 0017 named in the legs report: a leg leaves a child of the other axis only after the child's rank. Closing the gap needs side
nodes that may stand beside their child (in its rank, on the side the parent's flow leaves by), not a different weighting.
