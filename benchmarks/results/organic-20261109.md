# Organic layout against d3-force and Cytoscape CoSE, 2026-11-09

Branch `feature/single-pass-mixed` at `6192c8d`, Apple M3 (8 cores), Node 24.21, release build of `layout-command`; load average
about 2.5–3 during the run (not idle: times are indicative). Command: `node benchmarks/eval/organic.mjs --sizes 500,1000,2000
--out …` (from `benchmarks/eval`). Raw: [organic-20261109.json](organic-20261109.json).

## Task

Flat graphs, every node 180 × 54: the five organic corpus views (25–300 nodes, generator `organic_ladder`) and three sparse
synthetic graphs of 500, 1000 and 2000 nodes (a random tree plus 0.3 chords per node, seeded in the script). Engines:
Layerweave's organic placement (default, and `sparseStress` up to 500 nodes), d3-force 3.0.1 (300 ticks, seed 42) and Cytoscape
3.34.4 CoSE (1000 iterations), with the settings of `compare.mjs --flat-force`. Every drawing is measured with straight edges
between the node borders: node overlaps, crossings and line–node hits from the shared `metrics` operation; edge length CV,
normalized stress against graph distances (at the best scale), angular resolution, closest pair gap (over the mean edge length)
and area per node box, computed in the script. Layerweave runs as one process per call (its times include process start and
JSON); d3-force and CoSE run in the script's process.

(25 and 50 nodes and 1000 nodes in the raw file; the picture is the same.)

- **Quality up to 300 nodes:** Layerweave draws without overlaps, with a fifth to a tenth of d3-force's stress, a fifth of its
  crossings at 300 nodes and a fifth of its area, and the best angular resolution. d3-force spreads widely (40 node boxes of
  area per node) and has the more even edge lengths on 100 and 200 nodes; CoSE overlaps nodes from 25 on.
- **Time:** Layerweave grows steeply: 0.13 s at 100, 5.8 s at 200, 13.5 s at 300 nodes, against 0.06–0.22 s for d3-force.
  A sample of the 300-node run puts about 80 % of the time in the geometry repair after the stress placement
  (`repair::RootDrawing::scan_from_cache`, with string comparisons), not in the stress itself.
- **Scale:** above 300 children per area the default placement refuses; `sparseStress` (up to 500) takes 112 s on 500 nodes and
  leaves 431 overlaps. d3-force lays out 2000 nodes in 2.7 s without overlaps; CoSE takes 70 s and overlaps 3219 pairs.
