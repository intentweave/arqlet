## Stage 3 (restricted sides) and the reservation mechanism

The restricted policy is what the constructor applies: on a left-to-right area the source leaves on the right and the target
enters on the left (top-to-bottom: bottom and top). It is the `flowPorts` variant above: 229 constructed, 104 without a free
track, 21 edges that run backwards in the flow (NotForward). Those 21 are the cases where a policy with free sides (stage 1 or 2)
would be needed; the monotone constructor cannot build them.

Reservation mechanism that already exists: the snapshot carries `corridor_gaps` (before, after, extra width). The layout
widens corridors this way for routes that need room, and a later edit starts its areas with these widths. A reservation of
tracks can therefore use the same field: the missing tracks of a corridor become an extra width, instead of a new structure.
