## Mixed axes in `singlePassAuto`

The pre-check of the automatic choice rejected views with containers of mixed `lr`/`tb` axes. Over five seeds every one of the 11
mixed datasets is now better than the refined default ([idle limit](single-pass-idle-20261108.md)), so the pre-check takes them.

`single-pass-auto-check.mjs` (31 unmodified datasets, 150 nodes): **single pass 16, fallback 15, problems 0** (before: 5 / 26 / 0).
The 15 fallbacks: 14 with `straight` routing (organic and tree placement, ADR 0016 lets them stay on the other pipeline) and one
flattened view or view budget.

## Self-loops constructed

Single pass delegated every self-loop and drew it as a seed route. It now nests them at the node's freest corner, clear of every
route built, with the default's `nest_self_loops`; the loop's ports take the loop's ends. Only single pass changes; the other
chain methods of abstract routing keep delegating them.

Crossings, bends and hits are unchanged on every view (raw synthetic-2k: crossings 3039 → 3035). layerweave-code was regenerated by
a playground build between the runs and is left out of the comparison.

Default: byte-identical on all 31 datasets at threads 1 and 4.
