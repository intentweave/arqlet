## The changes of this step

- **Explicit ports** are planned like automatic ones (single pass); the construction delegates only those whose side the flow does
  not leave or enter by. fixed-ports: 4 → 3 delegated.
- **Areas that choose their direction** (`auto`): the construction prepared their edges without the chosen flow and delegated all
  of them (er-diagram: 11 of 12); it now takes the flow from the plan, and the ports of their edges take the sides of that flow.
  er-diagram: 0 delegated, shared lines 1 → 0.

Default: byte-identical on all 31 datasets at threads 1 and 4. Uniform views: unchanged except fixed-ports (one delegated edge
fewer) and layerweave-code (regenerated by a playground build in between).
