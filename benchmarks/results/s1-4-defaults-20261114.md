Apple M3, Darwin 25.0.0 arm64, rustc 1.99.0, Node 24.21.0; release `layout-command` from main after PR #22 (346171e) on
the branch `s1-4-defaults`. Command: `node benchmarks/eval/s1-4-defaults.mjs [--json]` (raw rows:
`s1-4-defaults-20261114.jsonl`). Every dataset in `benchmarks/datasets` except the very large ones (synthetic-20k,
protocol-*, overview-*, organic-300), bounded view of 150 nodes; one import per variant; elk-rs layered
(`@openedges/elk-layout`, pinned in `benchmarks/eval`) on the view the default selected, in the view's default
direction (it takes no per-container directions across a hierarchy). The machine was not idle: the times in the raw rows
are indicative only and no time is claimed here.

- **default**: as released (0.4.0 behaviour).

- **A + regionChildPorts**.
- **B**: `regionPorts`, `straightDetours`, `singlePassReversed` (A without `singlePassMixed`).
- **directionsFirst** alone (the options are not combined with it yet: the first matching branch wins).

The default against 0.4.0: the refinement fingerprints of the 15 views of the fingerprint sweep (1 and 4 threads) are
identical between v0.4.0 and main on the current datasets, and 26 of the stored rows of
`fingerprints-20261108-single-pass-default.jsonl` match (the other four: `layerweave-pipeline` is now
`arqlet-pipeline`, and `layerweave-code` drifted, 943/1737 against 941/1890).

Reading:

- A and B change only mixed graphs: on 30 of 31 datasets B is geometrically identical to the default (boxes and routes
  hashed), so the default's fingerprints would stay. The options act on regions, straight edges and reversed flows,
  which the layered, tree and organic datasets do not have.
- mixed-large: the default draws 44 lines through nodes. B draws none (crossings 811 against 721, bends 68 against 0,
  same area); A draws 4 (crossings 954, half the area); with `regionChildPorts` 2 (crossings 1145, more area). By the
  objective order (lines through nodes first) B is best; A trades crossings for area through `singlePassMixed`.
- can-bus: A bends less (27 against 32, through `singlePassMixed`); B is as the default (its buses keep `regionPorts`
  out).
- `regionChildPorts` is worse than A on mixed-large and mixed-layouts: not a default.
- directionsFirst acts where directions are free: er-diagram 15 against 23 bends (area +10 %), synthetic-2k 2430
  against 2792 crossings (area +72 %); elsewhere identical. It lays out twice where it acts.
- Against elk-rs (a different task: one direction for the whole view, no organic or tree areas): elk-rs crosses less on
  most layered views and is about half the area in sum (206 against 383 million where both ran); Arqlet crosses less on
  layerweave-code, synthetic-2k, network-vlan and petri-net, and draws trees and organic areas without bends. Area remains
  the largest gap.

Recommendation (for the decision, not applied): B as the default (it removes lines through nodes and leaves every other
measured view unchanged; `singlePassReversed` makes reversed flows draw as their mirrors, `regionPorts` keeps the border
contract on regions in layered parents), after a timing sweep on an idle machine. `singlePassMixed` and
`regionChildPorts` stay options; directionsFirst becomes a candidate once its time is measured and it combines with the
other options.
